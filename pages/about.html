Built for the people who grow our food.

AMIFY is a Nigerian marketplace that connects farmers directly to buyers.

AMPLIFY AGRICULTURE

The problem

Too many farmers sell their produce through a chain of middlemen and lose a big part of their earnings. Buyers pay more and often cannot tell where their food really comes from.

What AMIFY does

AMIFY puts farmers and buyers on the same platform. Farmers list what they grow, buyers order straight from them, and both sides deal directly with trusted sources.
